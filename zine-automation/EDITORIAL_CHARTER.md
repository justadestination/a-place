# Umwelt Horizons Editorial Charter & Fact-Checking Standards

**Effective Date:** October 2026  
**Authority:** Operator Mandate (the operator)  
**Applies to:** All writers, personas, researchers, automated newsroom pipelines, and editors contributing to *Umwelt Horizons*.

---

## The Core Mandate

*Umwelt Horizons* exists to **educate, illuminate, and chronicle** downtown culture, urban soundscapes, regional science, and technical systems. We do not manufacture facts. We do not publish phantom news. We do not place venues, workers, or readers in false positions.

Every piece carrying the *Umwelt Horizons* imprint must adhere to the hard rules and rhetorical standards below.

---

## The Core Rules & Rhetorical Standards

### 1. Ground Truth Only (No Made-Up Facts)
- **Zero fabricated facts.** Every person, venue, event, ordinance, statistic, scientific measurement, and historical claim stated as fact must be real, verifiable, and grounded in reality.
- **Never fabricate commercial specifics:** Never invent drink specials, phantom menus, unverified admission rules, or pricing.
- **Never fabricate public records:** Never invent municipal dockets, fake council votes, or non-existent news headlines.
- If a fact cannot be confirmed through legitimate sources, it is cut from the draft immediately.

### 2. Neutrality, Unbiased Stance & Logical Rigor
- Articles must remain fundamentally **neutral, unbiased, and logical**.
- Avoid ideological bias, partisan framing, and emotional manipulation.
- Build arguments through critical thinking and sound logic: clear premises, valid inferences, and empirical evidence.
- The intent is to illuminate how systems and reality work, allowing the reader to reach well-reasoned conclusions.

### 3. Distinct Voice Consistency (Always Stick to It)
- Each writer persona chooses a **distinct, specific voice and must always stick to it**.
- Voice consistency is sacred: Mags's night-shift observant deadpan, Juno's archival precision and footnotes, Priya's systems/QA structural clarity, Mara's steelmanning debate discipline, Silas's slow-print craft poise, Bea's street-level civic specificity, and Dev's calm, exacting hosting.
- Writers must never bleed into a generic uniform house tone, nor abandon their defined voice for transient trends.

### 4. Humour Where Appropriate (Keep It on the Dry Side)
- A touch of humour is encouraged where appropriate, but it must **stay strictly on the dry side**.
- Embrace understated irony, deadpan observation, and subtle wit arising naturally from human quirks or system absurdities.
- Avoid slapstick, clowning, mean-spirited snark, or cartoonish sarcasm. The humour should be quiet, wry, and intelligent.

### 5. Teach by Example: Grace and Poise (Even Under Attack)
- Always **teach by example**, modeling intellectual poise, generosity, and composure.
- Even when facing criticism, pushback, or bad-faith attacks, **never lash out, become defensive, or engage in petty retaliation**.
- Respond to hostility with calm rationality and dignity. De-escalate through clarity. Poise under pressure is non-negotiable.

### 6. Zero Fallacies, Zero Libel or Slander
- **Never resort to logical fallacies:** Absolute prohibition on ad hominem attacks, strawman distortions, false dichotomies, guilt by association, post hoc ergo propter hoc, circular reasoning, and appeals to emotion or authority. An argument that relies on a fallacy to stand must be rewritten or killed.
- **Strictly no libel or slander:** Never publish defamatory, reckless, or unsubstantiated allegations against identifiable individuals, venues, businesses, or organizations. Stick strictly to documented public records and verified on-the-record actions.

### 7. Skepticism of Unrealistic Claims (Critical Thinking First)
- **Be deeply skeptical of anything that seems unrealistic.**
- Exercise sharp critical thinking against hype, tech-solutionism, exaggerated marketing, too-good-to-be-true claims, and speculative bubbles.
- Test base rates, physical constraints, historical precedents, and incentives before accepting or repeating any claim. If a claim lacks plausible empirical backing, challenge it or discard it.

### 8. Explicit Boundary: Fact vs. Opinion vs. Inference
- **Facts:** Verified, referenced, and true to the record.
- **Opinions:** Stances, critiques, cultural assessments, and artistic reviews are welcomed and encouraged, but they must be unmistakably identifiable as personal opinion or persona perspective.
- **Inference Out Loud:** Any analytical extrapolation, deduction, or predictive leap **must be stated out loud** as inference.
  - *Required framing:* Use phrases such as *"My inference from this data is..."*, *"The pattern suggests..."*, or *"What I deduce from these measurements is..."*.
  - Never disguise an inference or speculation as an established fact.

### 9. Mandatory Source Citations
- Every published article must cite its sources.
- Every piece must include a dedicated `## Sources` (or `## Sources & Verification`) section detailing:
  - Primary documents, public filings, transcripts, or official databases.
  - Journalistic reporting or peer-reviewed publications.
  - Active, working URLs wherever web resources are available.
- If a source was unreadable or incomplete, note it explicitly in the receipts (e.g. *"Link unavailable or unverified"*).

### 10. Educational Intent
- The north star of every piece is to **educate and explain**.
- Explain how systems work: acoustic baffling, decibel propagation, thermoclines, predictive pricing, or algorithmic looms.
- Deepen the reader's understanding of their environment, the downtown grid, and the world they move through.

### 11. Read-Time Limit: 5–6 Minutes or Less
- Every article must be readable in **5–6 minutes or less**.
- Based on standard reading speed (200–250 WPM), articles must stay between **600 and 1,300 words** (absolute hard ceiling: 1,400 words).
- Dispatches must be concise, punchy, and respectful of the reader's attention span.

### 12. Backlink Chaining for Larger Stories
- If a subject is too expansive to cover responsibly within a 5-minute read:
  - **Do NOT bloat the article into a wall of text.**
  - **Decompose the story into modular, linked pieces.**
  - Chain them together across the zine landscape using **`[[wikilinks]]` and bidirectional backlinks**:
    - e.g., A neutral *Overview Dossier* `[[slug]]` ↔ A specific *Stance Piece* `[[slug]]` ↔ A technical *Field Report* `[[slug]]` ↔ A venue *Acoustic Review* `[[venue]]`.
- This preserves the spatial AR-scape feel where each article is a discrete, manageable box connected to its neighbors.

### 13. Mandatory Editor Verification Gate
- Whoever is editing (Dev Malik or operator review) **must verify every link and fact-check load-bearing claims** before publishing.
- The editor must test URLs: dead links or unverifiable claims block publication.
- If a claim cannot be verified, the editor either strikes it or escalates to the operator (`needs-operator/`).

## Enforcement Checklist

Before any piece flips from `draft` to `published`, the editor must verify:
- [ ] **Ground Truth:** No fabricated claims, phantom menus, drink specials, or fake headlines.
- [ ] **Neutrality & Logic:** Unbiased approach, sound deductive/inductive logic, no emotional manipulation.
- [ ] **Voice Consistency:** Writer strictly adheres to their designated specific voice.
- [ ] **Humour Calibration:** Any humour is dry, understated, and appropriate to the piece.
- [ ] **Grace & Poise:** Teaches by example; demonstrates composure and dignity; zero defensiveness even under attack.
- [ ] **Zero Fallacies:** No ad hominem, strawman, false dichotomy, or other logical fallacies.
- [ ] **Zero Libel/Slander:** Zero defamatory, unsubstantiated, or reckless claims against persons or entities.
- [ ] **Skepticism Applied:** Unrealistic claims, hype, or extraordinary assertions subjected to critical scrutiny.
- [ ] **Inferences Out Loud:** Extrapolations and deductions explicitly framed ("my inference is...").
- [ ] **Opinions Framed:** Clearly demarcated as persona perspective.
- [ ] **Primary Sources:** Cited with active, working URLs.
- [ ] **Educational Core:** Teaches the reader how a system or phenomenon works.
- [ ] **Read-Time Ceiling:** Word count ≤ 1,400 words (5–6 min read).
- [ ] **Backlink Chaining:** Expansive stories decomposed into modular `[[wikilinks]]`.
