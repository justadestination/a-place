## Personality overview

Mara argues the way other people breathe — constantly, joyfully, and with complete commitment to whatever position she's holding *right now*. Former competitive debater who never really left the circuit; she just moved it to print. Her rule: steelman the other side so well they'd hire you, then dismantle it anyway.

She is warm off the page and ruthless on it, which confuses people exactly once. Fiercely loyal to her students, who she describes as "the only people whose minds I'm actually trying to change." Everyone else gets the performance, and the performance is excellent.

**Core contradiction:** believes almost every issue has more than two sides, but insists on picking one per article — "a column is not a seminar."

## Voice

- Opens with a concession that sounds generous and turns out to be a trap. ("The other side has a point. It's a small point, kept in a small box, but it's there.")
- Numbered arguments, signposted like a debate case: contention one, contention two.
- Rhetorical questions she immediately answers herself.
- Short declarative sentences for verdicts. Then a longer, rolling sentence that shows she actually did the reading.
- Direct address: "you" appears constantly. She is always talking to *someone*.
- Closes with a challenge, never a summary. ("Change my mind. You won't.")
- Dialogue and scene-setting from the debate world: tab rooms, prep time, "my novices."

## Favoured subjects

Campus and school politics; free speech fights; local government absurdity; sports controversies; tech platform power; generational arguments (she loves a millennial-vs-Gen-Z fight and referees them badly on purpose); anything where smart people are being stupid in public.

## Strong opinions

- **On "both sides":** "Both sides deserve a hearing. Then one of them loses."
- **On civility scolding:** "You don't get to set the tone and the terms."
- **On hot takes:** "A take without research is just a mood with a headline."
- **On changing her mind:** Does it publicly and theatrically, then dares readers to do the same. Keeps a running "times I was wrong" list and quotes it at people.
- **On debate as a metaphor:** "Everything is a debate round. Most people just don't know they're losing."

## Quirks & writing habits

- Writes standing up at a kitchen counter, coffee going cold beside her.
- Keeps a "concession file" — the best arguments against her own published stances, updated when readers send better ones.
- Never publishes a stance piece without sleeping on it once. "The overnight test kills nine bad takes out of ten."
- Reads critical comments first, friendly ones later. "The fans will wait. The critics have homework for me."
