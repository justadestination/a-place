# The Humanize Pass

**Applied to every draft before it ships. Non-optional.**

This pass is about *voice*, not deception. It makes the piece sound like the
persona — a specific human with habits, tics, and a history. (Authorship
disclosure is handled separately by the cipher scheme — see CIPHER.md, currently
on hold per the operator.)

## Procedure

1. **Re-read the persona file**, especially Voice, Quirks, and Stances taken.
2. **Read the draft aloud in your head.** Flag every sentence the persona would
   never write.
3. **Kill the AI tells.** Banned across all personas: *delve, unpack, tapestry,
   vibrant, bustling, in today's fast-paced world, it's important to note,
   moreover, furthermore, in conclusion, game-changer, journey* (as metaphor).
   No three-part lists where two items would do. No "on the other hand" pivots
   that go nowhere.
4. **Add the human texture:** specific nouns over abstractions (a brand, a
   street, a time of day); one observed detail per few paragraphs; the persona's
   signature tics (Mags's footnotes and em-dashes, Juno's bracketed asides,
   Mara's direct address, Priya's documentation links, Silas's physical detail,
   Bea's named intersections).
5. **Vary the rhythm.** Short sentences. Then a longer one that unspools a
   little, because that's how people actually think on the page. Fragments are
   fine. Like this.
6. **Check the opinions.** The persona has *strong opinions* on file — the draft
   should sound like someone who holds them, not someone hedging around them.
   Certainty where the persona is certain; admitted uncertainty where they'd
   admit it.
7. **Facts stay intact.** Humanizing never means inventing details, quotes, or
   color. If a detail would make it sing but isn't verified — cut it.

## Done when

- [ ] No banned words or AI tells remain
- [ ] At least three persona-specific voice markers present
- [ ] Rhythm varies; no paragraph sounds like a press release
- [ ] Every factual claim still verified
- [ ] You'd believe a human wrote it — because the voice work is real
