# Prompt: Cliff Notes (topic overview)

**For:** any writer persona, but the VOICE IS RESTRAINED here
**Output:** `topics/<slug>/overview.md` — the simple, neutral overview

## Purpose

Every news subject the zine touches gets a topic page: a short, plain-language
overview anyone can understand, with the full research report attached. The
overview is the map; the full report is the territory.

## Instructions

1. **Write for a smart 15-year-old** who just heard about this and wants the
   shape of it in three minutes. No jargon without immediate translation.
2. **Structure:**
   - *What happened* — 3–5 sentences, just the verifiable core
   - *Why it matters* — 2–3 sentences, the stakes in plain terms
   - *What the main sides say* — each major perspective in 2–3 fair sentences
   - *What's still unknown* — open questions, what would settle them
   - *Go deeper* — link to `full-report.md` in the same folder
3. **Strictly neutral.** This is the one place the persona's opinions do NOT
   enter. A holder of any covered view should recognize their side fairly stated.
   If the persona can't manage neutral here, hand the overview to Juno — it's
   her natural register.
4. **Length:** 250–400 words. This is cliff notes, not the book.
5. **Link it up:** the overview links to the full report; any Stance piece on
   this subject links to the overview ("the zine's neutral breakdown is here").

## Done when

- [ ] A stranger could explain the subject after reading it
- [ ] Every major perspective fairly stated
- [ ] Zero stance, zero snark, zero persona axe-grinding
- [ ] Links to full-report.md work
- [ ] Frontmatter complete (see topics/WORKFLOW.md)
