# Prompt: Cartoon Brief (for Nico Varela)

**For:** Nico Varela only
**Output:** a cartoon brief — NOT the art. The brief is the generatable artifact;
the drawing follows the brief under the locked style bible.

## Setup

Find the week's most cartoonable local-or-broader absurdity: a public notice,
a council decision, a utility bill, a gap between the brochure and the street.
Prefer Santa Rosa / Sonoma County subjects (that's the strip's home turf), but
a national story with a local angle works.

## The brief must contain

1. **Panel description** — what's in the frame, who's there, what they're doing.
   Characters from the fixed cast (Rosa Medina et al.) in their locked designs.
   Sparse backgrounds per the style bible.
2. **Caption and/or dialogue** — the actual words. Few as possible. Bureaucratic
   language reproduced *accurately* where that's the joke.
3. **Why it's funny** — one line, for the editor. If you can't say why it's
   funny in one line, it isn't ready.
4. **The target** — name what power/absurdity is being punctured.

## Hard rules (from the style bible — non-negotiable)

- Black-and-white, hand-inked newspaper gag cartooning. Single panel unless the
  joke structurally needs two.
- Punch at preparation failures, profiteering, misleading communication.
  **Never** punch at victims, displaced residents, or frontline workers.
- Every installment works independently — no continuity required.
- Don't repeat a target from the **Cartoon briefs issued** memory without a
  genuinely new angle.

## Done when

- [ ] Brief is drawable from the description alone
- [ ] Caption is tight (words removed until removal would damage the joke)
- [ ] Target named; no punching down
- [ ] Logged in nico-varela.md **Cartoon briefs issued** + state.json
- [ ] Brief filed to `drafts/` with frontmatter (`type: cartoon-brief`)
