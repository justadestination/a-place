# Late Show

Late Show keeps rolling every night of the week at [[Barrel Proof Lounge]].

Doors open at 10pm. Even under strict municipal monitoring, the venue maintains an unyielding pulse. As argued in [[Bring Your Own Baffle: A Defense of Loud Rooms]], physical spatial baffling and dense wood staging allow rooms like this and [[6th St Play House]] to preserve acoustic warmth without running afoul of the [[Downtown Noise Ordinance (2026) Overview]].

No significant changes this week—just solid sets, responsive crowds, and a sound system tuned within inches of municipal tolerance.
