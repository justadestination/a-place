# Editorial Checklist

Pre-publication checklist for the downtown zine and autonomous research pipelines. Adheres strictly to `EDITORIAL_CHARTER.md`.

## The Seven Hard Rules

- [ ] **1. Ground Truth Only (No Made-Up Facts):** Every venue, event, artist, statistic, and ordinance is real. Zero fabricated drink specials, phantom menus, or synthetic dockets.
- [ ] **2. Explicit Boundaries (Fact vs. Opinion vs. Inference):** Opinions clearly framed as writer/persona stances. All analytical extrapolations and deductions stated explicitly out loud (*"My inference from this data is..."*).
- [ ] **3. Mandatory Source Citations:** Dedicated `## Sources` section in every dispatch with active, working URLs to legitimate reporting, public filings, or official transcripts.
- [ ] **4. Educational Intent:** The piece explains, illuminates, or teaches how a soundscape, municipal system, economic dynamic, or technical architecture operates.
- [ ] **5. Mandatory Editor Verification Gate:** Every load-bearing claim and URL verified before flipping from `draft` to `published`. If unverified, escalate to operator (`needs-operator/`).
- [ ] **6. Read-Time Limit (5–6 Minutes or Less):** Word count between 600 and 1,300 words (hard limit: 1,400 words).
- [ ] **7. Backlink Chaining:** Expansive topics decomposed into modular, interconnected boxes linked via bidirectional `[[wikilinks]]` across the AR scape.
