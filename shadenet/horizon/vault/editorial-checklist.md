# Editorial Checklist

Pre-publication checklist for the downtown zine and autonomous research pipelines. Adheres strictly to `EDITORIAL_CHARTER.md`.

## The Core Rules & Rhetorical Standards

- [ ] **1. Ground Truth Only (No Made-Up Facts):** Every venue, event, artist, statistic, and ordinance is real. Zero fabricated drink specials, phantom menus, or synthetic dockets.
- [ ] **2. Neutrality, Unbiased Stance & Logical Rigor:** Construct arguments on facts and sound logic. Zero logical fallacies (no ad hominem, strawman, or false dilemmas).
- [ ] **3. Distinct Voice Consistency:** Writer strictly adheres to their designated specific voice without drifting.
- [ ] **4. Dry Humour Calibration:** Humour is understated, dry, and observational where appropriate; zero slapstick, clowning, or mean snark.
- [ ] **5. Teach by Example (Grace and Poise):** Model composure and dignity even under attack; zero defensiveness or retaliation.
- [ ] **6. Zero Libel or Slander:** Absolutely no defamatory, reckless, or unsubstantiated claims against real individuals or businesses.
- [ ] **7. Skepticism of the Unrealistic:** Interrogate hype, extraordinary promises, and unrealistic claims with sharp critical thinking.
- [ ] **8. Explicit Boundaries (Fact vs. Opinion vs. Inference):** Opinions clearly framed as writer/persona stances. All analytical extrapolations and deductions stated explicitly out loud (*"My inference from this data is..."*).
- [ ] **9. Mandatory Source Citations:** Dedicated `## Sources` section in every dispatch with active, working URLs to legitimate reporting, public filings, or official transcripts.
- [ ] **10. Educational Intent:** The piece explains, illuminates, or teaches how a soundscape, municipal system, economic dynamic, or technical architecture operates.
- [ ] **11. Read-Time Limit (5–6 Minutes or Less):** Word count between 600 and 1,300 words (hard limit: 1,400 words).
- [ ] **12. Backlink Chaining:** Expansive topics decomposed into modular, interconnected boxes linked via bidirectional `[[wikilinks]]` across the AR scape.
- [ ] **13. Mandatory Editor Verification Gate:** Every load-bearing claim and URL verified before flipping from `draft` to `published`. If unverified, escalate to operator (`needs-operator/`).
