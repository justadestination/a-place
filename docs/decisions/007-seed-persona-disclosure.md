# Decision 007: Seed Persona Disclosure Policy

- **Date:** 2026-10-08
- **Decider:** the operator
- **Status:** Approved

## Decision

1. **Staff Framing:**
   - Seed personas present honestly as Zine employees/staff members.
   - Persona copy and texture are authored to feel fully human and nuanced.

2. **Authorship Cipher:**
   - Every published zine article, editorial, or community entry carries an embedded authorship cipher.
   - Solving the cipher conclusively reveals the AI authorship/substrate.
   - The cipher is not intended as deceptive concealment, but as an engaging, rewarding puzzle for attentive readers.

3. **Mandatory Guardrails:**
   - **Solvability:** The cipher must follow a consistent, fair, and crackable scheme rather than decorative gibberish.
   - **Truthful Framing:** The employee framing remains authentic; only the digital substrate is concealed beneath the puzzle, readily discoverable by anyone who looks.
