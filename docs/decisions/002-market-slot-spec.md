# Decision 002: Market Slot Spec (Serendipilyst)

- **Date:** 2026-10-08
- **Decider:** the operator
- **Status:** Approved

## Decision

1. **Market Concept:**
   - Serendipilyst functions as an anonymous classifieds and exchange board adjacent to Craigslist and Facebook Marketplace.
   - Purpose: An anonymous, community-driven venue for listing sales, services, odd jobs, and offerings.

2. **Ingestion & Flag Collection:**
   - Users and venues can post offers directly or anchor them as "flags" on their respective nodes/exposures.
   - Serendipilyst serves as the central aggregation feed where all distributed flags and listings are collected and discovered.

3. **Settlement & Transactions:**
   - 100% off-platform settlement.
   - All transactions occur peer-to-peer (cash in person, direct exchange, barter).
   - Zero on-platform payment processing, escrow, fees, or cuts. ShadeNet acts purely as an anonymous discovery and matchmaker layer.

4. **Identity & Privacy:**
   - Anonymous posting by default.
   - Initial contact and inquiry mediated through anonymous flags and node inboxes (Boxes / Towncrier).
